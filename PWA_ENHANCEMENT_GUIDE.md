## 📱 **How to Install on Your Devices**

### **Android Phones/Tablets:**
1. Open **Chrome** browser
2. Go to: `https://astrobsm-order-placement-fykxb.ondigitalocean.app/`
3. Look for **"Add to Home Screen"** popup, OR
4. Tap **⋮** (3-dot menu) → **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Choose name: **"ASTRO-BSM Orders"**
6. Tap **"Add"**

### **iPhones/iPads:**  
1. Open **Safari** browser (must be Safari!)
2. Go to: `https://astrobsm-order-placement-fykxb.ondigitalocean.app/`
3. Tap **Share** button (□↗) at bottom
4. Scroll and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Choose name: **"ASTRO-BSM Orders"**
6. Tap **"Add"**

### **Desktop/Laptop:**
1. Open **Chrome** browser
2. Go to: `https://astrobsm-order-placement-fykxb.ondigitalocean.app/`
3. Look for **install icon** ⬇️ in address bar
4. Click to install as desktop app
